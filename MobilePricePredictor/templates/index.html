About this project

This application trains machine-learning regressors on the uploaded 836-row mobile phone dataset. Brand and model choices are taken directly from the dataset.

Forecast limitation: the source data contains no dated historical price series. Future prices are scenario projections using the annual depreciation assumption selected by the user.

Price alert limitation: in-app alerts compare the model's estimated price against your target; they are not live retailer price tracking.
